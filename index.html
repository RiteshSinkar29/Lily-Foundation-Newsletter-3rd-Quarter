Foundation Day Celebration
Lily Foundation Celebrates 26 Years of a Noble Journey
Lily Foundation began its noble journey on 25th August 2000, with a steadfast commitment to bringing the light of education to those who need it most. Every year, 25th August is observed as Lily Foundation Day, commemorating the beginning of this remarkable mission.
This year marked a proud milestone as Lily Foundation completed 26 years of its continuous service towards a noble cause-providing the gift of education and helping break the cycle of poverty through knowledge and opportunity.
To mark this special occasion, all the hubs of Lily Foundation enthusiastically hoisted the Lily Foundation flag and organized various cultural programmes to commemorate the day. Students from the respective hubs actively participated in the celebrations and presented a variety of cultural performances, adding colour, enthusiasm and joy to the occasion. A locally made Lily Birthday song with best lyrics and music was presented at Narrah Institute of Education drawing special attention.
 
Recognition from Akhil Bharat Purva Sainik Seva Parishad
Lily Foundation Honoured with “Ghatsila Gaurav Samman”
On the occasion of Kargil Vijay Diwas on 26 July, Lily Foundation was honoured with the prestigious “Ghatsila Gaurav Samman” by the Akhil Bharatiya Purva Sainik Seva Parishad at Moubhandar Copper Club, Ghatsila.
The award was presented in recognition of Lily Foundation’s outstanding contribution to society through its various community welfare initiatives, which was received by Arup Choudhury Secretary of The Lily Foundation. The Foundation has been working consistently towards social development through programmes in educational scholarships, healthcare, blood donation camps, and several other welfare activities.

New Vocational Centre at DMS Institute Dhulai
Electrical House Wiring Vocational Course at DMS Institute
A new vocational training wing on Electrical House Wiring has been launched at DMS Institute, Dhulai, Bankura, as part of the Foundation’s efforts to equip young learners with practical and income-generating skills on 8th August 2026.
Through this course, students will receive hands-on training in house wiring, basic electrical work, and the repair and maintenance

Achiever’s Corner
From Training to Self-Reliance: A Journey of Empowerment
Merajun Parwin

Merajun Parwin, a resident of Muslim Basti, Kashida, Ghatsila, comes from a family of 6. Her father, a small business owner, earns only enough to meet the family’s basic needs. Pratima Manki, from Dharambahal, belongs to a family of 4, where her father works as a daily wage labourer and struggles to make ends meet.

Both came to know about the Health General Duty Assistant Course, a vocational training programme at VIYE, Ghatsila, and enrolled themselves with the hope of building a better future. After successfully completing the course, they secured employment at Upasam Diagnostic Centre, enabling them to earn independently and contribute to their families’ financial well-being.

Pratima Manki

Their journey is a testament to how vocational education can break the cycle of poverty, create opportunities, and empower young people to become self-reliant.
What makes their story even more inspiring is that their journey has come full circle. Today, they are giving back by assisting in Lily Women’s Care Camps (Maternity Camps) organised at VIYE, Ghatsila.

Lily Foundation wishes Merajun & Pratima very rewarding future years.

Lily Women’s Care Expands to Purulia
Following the grand success of the Lily Women’s Care (Maternity Camp) at Ghatsila, Lily Foundation has extended the initiative to Purulia, where access to medical facilities remains limited.
The monthly camp, held at Yugacharya Vivekananda Seva Samity, Boro, provides free medical consultation and treatment to pregnant women. Beneficiaries are also provided with medicines, protein drinks, almonds, nuts, and other nutritional supplements to support maternal health.
With this initiative, Lily Foundation is taking essential healthcare services closer to communities that need them most.
